- [Validation and day 2 pipelines](../../automation/02-cicd-pipelines/#validation-and-day-2-pipelines): the validation of [Part 7](../07-validation-handover/run-validation-in-a-pipeline.mdx) and the day 2 operations of [Part 8](../08-day2-operations/run-day2-in-a-pipeline.mdx), on each platform. Two keys of `infrastructure.yml` belong to them: `devops.pipelines.environments.operations` (the one approval environment of every day 2 change, the variable `AZL_ENVIRONMENT_OPERATIONS`) and `devops.pipelines.schedules.check_for_updates` (when the check for updates runs: a five-field cron expression in UTC, default `17 4 * * *`)

## Why This Changed

- The Implementation section should stay focused on hands-on deployment execution.
- The Automation section is now the canonical home for CI/CD platforms, pipeline setup, and runner guidance.
- Keeping CI/CD details in one place reduces duplication and documentation drift.
